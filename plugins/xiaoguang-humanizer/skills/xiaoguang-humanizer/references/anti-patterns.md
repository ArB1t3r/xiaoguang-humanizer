# 共用反模式 / Shared anti-patterns

24 类反模式，按“形态 → 修法 → 中英例子”使用。例子均为新写的教学材料，里面的事实不能移植到用户文章。引文、专名、代码及用户指定的形式按主文件保护。

Use these 24 patterns as editing instructions. All examples are original teaching material. Their facts must not migrate into the user's writing.

## C01 空泛开场 / Generic scene-setting

**形态：** 在飞速发展的时代、本文将深入探讨；in today's fast-paced world, this comprehensive guide explores.
**修法 / Move：** 删掉适用于任何主题的前奏，从实际问题或已有场景起笔。Start with the subject, action, or supplied scene.

- 中：在日新月异的时代，排班的重要性不言而喻。本周有两人请假，需要调整排班。 → 本周有两人请假，需要调整排班。
- EN: In today's demanding workplace, scheduling matters more than ever. Two people are off this week, so we need to revise the rota. → Two people are off this week, so we need to revise the rota.

## C02 空洞拔高 / Inflated significance

**形态：** 里程碑、开启新篇章、重塑行业；pivotal, transformative, a testament to.
**修法 / Move：** 陈述事件，只写有材料支持的影响。State the event and supported consequences.

- 中：新增夜间服务窗口，为服务升级写下浓墨重彩的一笔。 → 新增了夜间服务窗口。
- EN: Adding an evening service window marks a transformative milestone in customer care. → An evening service window has been added.

## C03 假对比 / Hollow contrast

**形态：** 不只是 X，更是 Y；not merely X but Y. 前半句只为垫高后半句。
**修法 / Move：** 直接说有效信息；真正区分状态或纠正已提出的误解时保留对比。State the point; keep contrasts carrying distinct information.

- 中：这不只是一张时刻表，更是掌握行程的钥匙。它列出了末班车时间。 → 这张时刻表列出了末班车时间。
- EN: This is more than a timetable; it is a key to your journey. It lists the last departures. → The timetable lists the last departures.

## C04 与假想敌辩论 / Phantom objections

**形态：** 我不是在否定……；有人可能会说……；one might object, don't get me wrong.
**修法 / Move：** 删除没有对应争议的辩解，保留实际主张和真实反对意见。Remove invented opposition; retain real tradeoffs.

- 中：我并不是反对开会，也不是忽视沟通。我的建议是把周会改到周二。 → 我建议把周会改到周二。
- EN: This isn't an attack on meetings or a dismissal of communication. I suggest moving the weekly meeting to Tuesday. → I suggest moving the weekly meeting to Tuesday.

## C05 机械三项并列 / Decorative triads

**形态：** 创新、突破、跃迁；三个近义词冒充三条信息。
**修法 / Move：** 合并重复含义，真实三项全留，不凑两项或第四项。Merge redundancy; retain every distinct item.

- 中：此次调整实现提速、增效、效率跃升：报销只需一次审批。 → 调整后，报销只需一次审批。
- EN: The change brings speed, efficiency, and productivity: expenses now need one approval. → Expenses now need one approval.

## C06 等形段落 / Repeated paragraph molds

**形态：** 每段都“主题句→解释→升华”；论证不推进，换段落顺序也没有影响。
**修法 / Move：** 按问题、证据、后果或时间组织；独立资料允许并列。Organize by actual relationships; do not force dependencies between reference entries.

- 中：名单尚未确认。这说明准备工作很重要。印刷需要名单。这说明协作很重要。 → 印刷需要名单，但名单尚未确认。
- EN: The guest list is unconfirmed. Preparation matters. Printing requires the list. Coordination matters. → Printing requires the guest list, which is still unconfirmed.

## C07 换词复述 / Repetition disguised as explanation

**形态：** 换句话说、也就是说；in other words, essentially. 后句不增加信息。
**修法 / Move：** 留一次清楚表述；真正有助理解的解释保留。Keep one clear statement and explanations that actually help.

- 中：登记截至周五。换句话说，周五就是登记的截止日期。 → 登记截至周五。
- EN: Registration closes Friday. In other words, Friday is the registration deadline. → Registration closes Friday.

## C08 空洞两面论 / Empty balancing

**形态：** 机遇与挑战并存；offers opportunities but also presents challenges.
**修法 / Move：** 写材料中的收益、代价和条件，删掉空的平衡姿态。Name the supplied tradeoff.

- 中：搬迁有机遇也有挑战。新办公室租金低，但离车站远。 → 新办公室租金低，但离车站远。
- EN: Relocation brings opportunities and challenges. The new office costs less but is farther from the station. → The new office costs less but is farther from the station.

## C09 伪深刻与万能金句 / Manufactured profundity

**形态：** X 是 Y 的语言、真正的核心在于；X is the currency of Y, the deeper truth.
**修法 / Move：** 去掉包装，保留能解释的已有判断。含糊比喻不能凭空扩写成新论点。State the existing claim without inventing an interpretation.

- 中：协作是进度的语言。我们说的协作，就是编辑和设计师一起检查目录。 → 这里的协作是编辑和设计师一起检查目录。
- EN: Collaboration is the language of progress. By collaboration, we mean the editor and designer checking the contents together. → Here, collaboration means the editor and designer checking the contents together.

## C10 宣传形容词堆叠 / Promotional adjective piles

**形态：** 世界级、卓越、全方位、极致；world-class, unparalleled, seamless.
**修法 / Move：** 用已有功能或体验说明价值。营销稿仍可有吸引力，不新增承诺。Use supplied features and benefits.

- 中：这款卓越、强大、极致易用的灯具支持三档亮度。 → 这盏灯有三档亮度。
- EN: This unparalleled, powerful, effortlessly intuitive lamp has three brightness settings. → The lamp has three brightness settings.

## C11 空泛权威 / Unspecified authority

**形态：** 专家普遍认为、研究显示、广受认可；experts agree, studies prove.
**修法 / Move：** 保留具体归因；缺来源时指出缺口，不把转述改成已证实事实。Use known attribution; flag missing evidence without laundering a claim.

- 中：这项方案获得广泛认可。材料只引述顾问林的意见：“可以试点。” → 顾问林认为这项方案“可以试点”。
- EN: The plan has broad support. The material quotes only consultant Lin: “It is ready for a pilot.” → Consultant Lin says the plan “is ready for a pilot.”

## C12 模糊关系 / Vague connections

**形态：** 与……有关、形成关联；connected with, associated with. 明知具体关系却绕开不说。
**修法 / Move：** 用已有角色和关系；没有资料则保持概括。Name the relationship only when known.

- 中：周与这份刊物有密切联系，她负责校对。 → 周负责这份刊物的校对。
- EN: Zhou is closely associated with the journal and handles its proofreading. → Zhou proofreads the journal.

## C13 不确定性叠床架屋 / Stacked hedging

**形态：** 也许可能、或许可以潜在地；could potentially possibly, might arguably.
**修法 / Move：** 保留实际不确定性，压掉同义重复。Keep the actual uncertainty once.

- 中：这项调整也许可能缩短等待时间，目前尚未验证。 → 这项调整可能缩短等待时间，目前尚未验证。
- EN: The change could potentially possibly reduce waiting time; this remains unverified. → The change may reduce waiting time; this remains unverified.

## C14 名词堆叠与行动者消失 / Noun piles and hidden actors

**形态：** 开展……的实施工作；implementation of the optimization of.
**修法 / Move：** 用动词和已知行动者。不知道谁做的，可以用被动句。Use verbs and known actors; do not invent an actor.

- 中：编辑部将开展对目录的核对工作。 → 编辑部将核对目录。
- EN: The editorial team will carry out the verification of the contents list. → The editorial team will check the contents list.

## C15 同义词轮换 / Synonym cycling

**形态：** 同一个对象连续换名称，指代变得不清楚。
**修法 / Move：** 固定术语和指代。Use stable terms rather than decorative synonyms.

- 中：申请人填写表格。该参与主体随后签名。此报名个体保留副本。 → 申请人填表并签名，然后保留副本。
- EN: The applicant fills in the form. The participating individual signs it. The registration subject keeps a copy. → The applicant fills in and signs the form, then keeps a copy.

## C16 万能标点与装饰强调 / Punctuation and emphasis doing all the work

**形态：** 每句靠破折号转折，每个短语加粗，标题堆表情。
**修法 / Move：** 按语义选标点，强调只用于定位信息。数字区间、减号、引文和代码保留。Use punctuation for syntax and emphasis for navigation.

- 中：**结果**——**已经确认**——**门票售罄**。 → 已确认门票售罄。
- EN: **The result**—**now confirmed**—**tickets are sold out**. → It is confirmed: tickets are sold out.

## C17 把文章写成提纲 / Prose reduced to labeled bullets

**形态：** 每句都是“标签：解释”，连贯叙事被拆碎。
**修法 / Move：** 连续叙述用段落；比较、步骤、清单保留结构。Use prose for connected ideas and lists for parallel items or steps.

- 中：背景：楼顶漏水。影响：漏水导致展览延期。 → 楼顶漏水，展览因此延期。
- EN: Background: the roof leaked. Impact: this delayed the exhibition. → The roof leak delayed the exhibition.

## C18 助手话术残留 / Assistant wrapper

**形态：** 当然可以！以下是……希望对你有帮助；certainly, here is your polished version.
**修法 / Move：** 删除交付正文里的服务话术。真实邮件问候和签名保留。Remove the wrapper, not the actual message.

- 中：当然可以！以下是通知：周六闭馆。希望对您有所帮助。 → 周六闭馆。
- EN: Certainly! Here is your notice: The gallery is closed Saturday. I hope this helps. → The gallery is closed Saturday.

## C19 编辑过程冒充内容 / Process narration

**形态：** 我已保留……并确保……；this section was added to improve. 正文解释编辑过程。
**修法 / Move：** 写读者需要的状态或结果；变更日志、交接和方法说明可保留过程。Describe the current subject; retain history where it is the document's purpose.

- 中：为提升说明的清晰度，本段已更新，注明预约提前两天开放。 → 预约提前两天开放。
- EN: This section has been updated for clarity to state that bookings open two days in advance. → Bookings open two days in advance.

## C20 引用与模板残留 / Citation and template residue

**形态：** 内部引用 token、未填姓名、失效脚注、查无出处的完整参考条目。
**修法 / Move：** 用提供的来源恢复引用；缺证据则标记待核，不能删掉标记便假装核实。URL 未授权不改。Resolve from supplied evidence or flag the gap.

- 中：会议在周四举行〔工具引用占位符〕。提供的议程明确写着周四。 → 议程注明会议在周四举行。
- EN: The meeting is on Thursday [tool citation placeholder]. The supplied agenda confirms Thursday. → The agenda confirms that the meeting is on Thursday.

## C21 刻意扮人 / Manufactured humanity

**形态：** 强塞“说实话”、虚构感受和经历、故意错字、无关插话。
**修法 / Move：** 用已有态度和细节表达声音，不新增人设。用户原有经历不擅自认定为虚构。Use the supplied perspective without inventing a persona.

- 中，原稿：我还没决定是否续订。错误改法：说真的，我昨夜三点还在纠结。正确改法：我还没决定要不要续订。
- EN, source: I have not decided whether to renew. Bad: Honestly, I was awake at 3 a.m. agonizing over it. Better: I haven't decided whether to renew.

## C22 通用乐观结尾 / Stock optimistic endings

**形态：** 未来可期、让我们共同迎接；exciting times ahead, the future looks bright.
**修法 / Move：** 在最后一个有效事实、决定或请求处结束。Stop at the last useful fact, decision, or request.

- 中：下一次测试安排在周二。让我们共同期待更加光明的未来。 → 下一次测试安排在周二。
- EN: The next test is on Tuesday. Exciting times lie ahead as we embrace the future. → The next test is on Tuesday.

## C23 过度防御与保证 / Unnecessary caveats and assurances

**形态：** 反复说“这并不意味着”“应谨慎理解”“我已确保质量”，却无对应争议。
**修法 / Move：** 删除无关的预防性解释，留下影响判断或行动的具体限制。Keep material qualifications; remove generic self-protection.

- 中：初稿已发出。这并不意味着所有写作问题都已彻底解决，也不应如此理解。还缺两张图。 → 初稿已发出，还缺两张图。
- EN: The draft has been sent. This should not be understood to mean that every writing issue has been definitively resolved. Two figures are still missing. → The draft has been sent; two figures are still missing.

## C24 空泛解读尾句 / Superficial interpretive tails

**形态：** ……彰显了／体现了；..., highlighting / underscoring / reflecting. 尾句只给事实贴标签。
**修法 / Move：** 删空泛解读，保留有证据的后果，不把同时发生写成因果。Cut the gloss; retain supported consequences.

- 中：阅览室新增了六把椅子，彰显了对阅读体验的深切关注。 → 阅览室新增了六把椅子。
- EN: Six chairs were added to the reading room, underscoring a profound commitment to the reading experience. → Six chairs were added to the reading room.

## 扫描顺序 / Review order

先看段落和论点，再看句型，最后看用词与标点。一处修改可以消除多类特征，不把清单变成 24 轮重写。Review structure, sentences, then wording and formatting. One edit can fix several patterns.
