# English rules / 英文专项规则

These six rules supplement the shared catalog and overlap with it. Use the prose's intended register and regional spelling. They also apply when a Chinese instruction asks you to edit an English draft.

## E01 Prefer direct predicates / 用直接谓语

Replace inflated substitutes for “is” and “has” when they add nothing. A verb such as “serves” is still useful when it describes an actual function.

Before: The annex stands as a dedicated space that boasts two reading rooms.

After: The annex has two reading rooms.

Keep: The kitchen serves lunch until 2 p.m.

Do not change a proposed capability into an existing one.

## E02 Replace stock diction by meaning / 按意思处理高频套词

Do not replace a watched word with a fancier synonym. Determine what the sentence means, then use the ordinary word or rebuild the clause. Preserve technical senses, direct quotations, names, and the writer's explicit choices.

| Watch in generic prose | Consider | Preserve when meaningful |
|---|---|---|
| delve into | examine, discuss, or the actual finding | A title or quoted phrase |
| leverage | use | Financial leverage or a precise mechanical sense |
| facilitate | help, enable, or a specific action | “Facilitate a workshop” when it identifies the role |
| utilize | use | Established quoted terminology |
| seamless | describe the actual transition | Do not invent “no setup required” |
| robust | describe the supported property | Robust statistics, a named method |
| vibrant tapestry | describe the actual subject | Literal textiles and deliberate literary imagery |
| underscore, highlight | state the relevant fact | Literal UI highlighting or sourced emphasis |
| multifaceted, comprehensive | name the actual scope | A documented scope that matters to the reader |
| in order to, due to the fact that | to, because | Preserve relationships and negation |

Before: We will leverage the shared calendar to facilitate room bookings.

After: We will use the shared calendar to book rooms.

Avoid “synergy → collaboration → alignment” cycling when the real issue is that the draft never says what people do.

## E03 Repair translation-shaped English / 修复英文翻译腔

Use idiomatic verbs and collocations. Do not translate Chinese abstract nouns one by one or import a Chinese publicity cadence into English.

Before: We will carry out an optimization of the usage experience of the application.

After: We will improve the experience of using the application.

The source supplies no measurable result, so do not invent one. A plain general statement is acceptable.

When translating, preserve obligation: “请于周五前提交” is a request with a deadline, not “You might consider submitting when convenient.”

## E04 Match register without performing informality / 自然但不装熟

Use contractions where they fit. Do not inject fragments, slang, rhetorical questions, first-person reactions, or “Honestly?” to make neutral prose sound personal. Preserve the author's supplied warmth or opinion.

Before: Here's the thing. Honestly? We cannot confirm the date until the venue replies.

After, work message: We can't confirm the date until the venue replies.

After, formal notice: We cannot confirm the date until the venue replies.

For academic or technical writing, keep defined terms, real uncertainty, and useful passive constructions. Do not turn “may be associated with” into “causes.”

## E05 Make typography serve the document / 格式服务文档

Follow the house style for title case, sentence case, quotation marks, and regional spelling. Reduce arbitrary boldface, slogan fragments, and punctuation chains. Do not normalize an existing heading if doing so would break an anchor without authorization.

Before: **A Bold New Chapter In Scheduling** — **The Future Starts Here**

After, when freely editing a draft heading: Scheduling

Keep numerical ranges such as “2019–2024,” negative signs, hyphenated technical terms, and punctuation inside code. Do not replace every dash with a colon: that just produces a new repetitive pattern.

## E06 Write for the actual reader / 按读者已有信息写

In a reply, lead with the answer or decision and retain the reason that changes the reader's action. Avoid re-explaining background the visible conversation already establishes. If there is no visible context, do not assume the reader knows it.

Context: The recipient has just said that Room B is unavailable on Monday.

Before: As you have explained, Room B is unavailable on Monday. Given the importance of ensuring that our meeting has a suitable venue, I would like to suggest Tuesday. Room B is available then.

After: Let's meet on Tuesday; Room B is available then.

For a standalone article, retain background the new reader needs. A reply and an article should not have the same opening.

## Full example / 完整例子

**Draft**

In today's rapidly evolving operational landscape, this update represents a pivotal step toward seamless collaboration. The finance team is launching a pilot on Wednesday that reduces expense approvals from two to one. Notably, claims over CNY 5,000 still require the budget owner's signature. This should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every concern has been fully addressed. The finance team will collect feedback after two weeks. The future of efficient teamwork looks bright.

**Rewrite**

From Wednesday, the finance team will trial one approval for expense claims, down from two. Claims over CNY 5,000 still require the budget owner's signature. The team will collect feedback after two weeks.

**Check**

The approval counts, start day, pilot status, amount threshold, signature requirement, feedback timing, and responsible team survive. The rewrite adds no claim about speed or guaranteed results.

## Bilingual alignment / 双语对齐

When producing both languages, verify the same names, amounts, thresholds, dates, conditions, attributions, and obligations. Write natural sentences independently; do not copy clause order or paragraph length mechanically. If supplied versions disagree about a deadline or number, identify the mismatch and ask only for the unresolved value; do not choose whichever sounds better.
